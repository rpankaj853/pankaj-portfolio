Privacy Policy — NodeTrail

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how NodeTrail ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile puzzle game, available on Google Play. NodeTrail is developed and published by Pankaj Rana.

1. Information We Collect

NodeTrail does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information on our own servers.

The following data is handled by the App:

  • Game progress data (completed levels, stars, coins, settings such as sound on/off) is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data — see Section 2 below.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

NodeTrail shows optional rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, Google may automatically collect certain device information, including:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse (approximate) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad performance diagnostics)

This collection is performed by Google under its own policies. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties. The only third party that processes data through the App is Google AdMob, as described in Section 2, solely for the purpose of serving advertisements.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App. We hold no copies of your data.

5. Children's Privacy

NodeTrail is a casual puzzle game suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google Play's Families and Ads policies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will assist.

6. Security

Since we do not operate servers or transmit your personal data, exposure risk is minimal. Ad traffic is handled by Google over encrypted connections.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
